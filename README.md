# Mockgent tasks

Mockgent's public project repo: tasks and the Neki ledger for Mockgent, working, stateful fakes of 254 real-world APIs (AWS, Stripe, OpenAI, Slack, GitHub and more). LocalStack, but for every API, open source for good. Built by Sayan Bhattacharya. It started as an [idea on Ideato](https://www.ideato.social/idea/4bb1032e-e6d7-46b4-9aac-e10c1de29f9a).

- [Issues](https://github.com/topc-social/mockgent-tasks/issues): open tasks. Each issue is one task, and its `neki:N` label is the Neki it earns (1 Neki = 1 rupee of work at market price).
- [`LEDGER.md`](LEDGER.md): who has earned what. Your share of Mockgent = your Neki / all Neki there.

Code: [tinker20/mockgent](https://github.com/tinker20/mockgent) (members only until it goes public).

## How it works

1. Pick any open issue. Anyone can hand in, and the best hand-in wins.
2. Do the work however you like, including with your own AI.
3. Hand in by commenting on the issue with a link others can open (usually a pull request on the code repo) and a line or two on what you did.
4. The owner accepts the best hand-in by closing the issue as completed. A workflow then labels it `accepted` and adds the two Neki rows (1% idea, 99% work) to [`LEDGER.md`](LEDGER.md) itself.

## Rules

- Proof must be something others can open, and anyone you quote or record has to agree to it.
- Made-up people, answers or numbers earn nothing.
- Neki is a record of your work and becomes your share in what gets built. Rules: TOPC's NEKI.md.
